# Demo Bank — Fees and Charges

## Current account
- Monthly account fee: £0
- Transfers to other Demo Bank customers: free
- UK Faster Payments: free
- International (SWIFT) payment: £15 per payment

## Debit card
- UK card purchases: free
- Purchases abroad: 2.75% non-sterling transaction fee
- Cash withdrawal abroad: 2.75% plus £1.50 per withdrawal
- Replacement card: free (first one each year), then £5

## Credit card
- Purchase APR: 22.9% representative
- Late payment fee: £12
- Over-limit fee: £12
- Cash advance fee: 3% (minimum £3)

## Overdraft
- Arranged overdraft interest: 39.9% EAR
- Unarranged overdraft: not permitted; payments will be declined
