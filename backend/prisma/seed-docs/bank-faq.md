# Demo Bank — Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I send money to someone?
Open the app, choose "Pay to", enter the recipient's username and the amount in GBP, then confirm. Transfers between Demo Bank customers are instant and free of charge.

## What is the daily transfer limit?
Standard customers can send up to £10,000 per day. Payments above £5,000 may require extra security checks.

## How do I block a lost or stolen card?
Call our 24/7 card line on 0800 000 0000 or block the card in the app under Cards > Manage. A blocked card cannot be used for new transactions. A replacement card arrives within 3–5 working days.

## When will a card refund appear on my account?
Refunds usually appear within 3–5 working days of the merchant processing them. They show as a CREDIT transaction.

## Is my money protected?
Eligible deposits are protected up to £85,000 per person by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). (Demo text only.)

## How do I open a new card?
Go to Cards > Add card and choose a debit or credit card. Your new card is active immediately for online use.
