# AI Banking — 30-Second Video Script

**Length:** 30 seconds
**Audience:** clients and business stakeholders (UK banking)
**Goal:** a quick teaser: everyday banking, an AI assistant that knows your account, and privacy built in.

**Before recording:** reset the demo data, log in as **alice**, and keep the **AI Assistant** page ready in a second tab.

---

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Alice's **POS Simulation** page: balance, cards, recent spending | "Meet AI Banking — everyday banking with an assistant that knows your account." |
| 0:05–0:11 | Click **Simulate random purchase**, then **Pay to** bob £10. Balance updates. | "Customers pay by card or send money to friends, and their balance updates instantly." |
| 0:11–0:20 | **AI Assistant**: ask *"How much did I spend at Tesco?"*, then *"What are the fees for using my card abroad?"* Point at the **Sources** line. | "They can ask questions in plain English, about their own spending or the bank's fees, and see exactly where each answer came from." |
| 0:20–0:26 | Log in as **bob**, ask *"What is my mortgage monthly payment?"*. It finds nothing. | "And every customer only ever sees their own data." |
| 0:26–0:30 | Closing slide: **AI Banking** | "AI Banking: simple, helpful, and private." |

---

## Full voiceover (about 75 words)

> "Meet AI Banking — everyday banking with an assistant that knows your account.
> Customers pay by card or send money to friends, and their balance updates instantly.
> They can ask questions in plain English, about their own spending or the bank's fees, and see exactly where each answer came from.
> And every customer only ever sees their own data.
> AI Banking: simple, helpful, and private."

## Tips

- Record the assistant's answers ahead of time and cut them in, so you don't wait on screen.
- Use a quick cut for the switch to Bob, not a full log-out and log-in.
